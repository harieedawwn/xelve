# Story — XELVE (v2: mystery, no family drama)

User feedback v1: hated the family/grief angle ("cố làm cho xúc động"). v2 rules:
- Mystery first. Nobody explains anything. No sad backstory, no relatives, no tears, no "mẹ/chị/em".
- Lines are cold, short, functional: logs, signals, labels, numbers. Never more than 10 words.
- Questions are planted, not answered in Chapter 1. Chapter 2 answers some, not all.
- Do → show → tell still applies. Rooms tell more than text.

## Premise
05:47. The city goes white. Colour leaves everything; what the white swallows, it rebuilds badly as **Bóng**: charcoal figures repeating whatever used to happen in that place. They hoard the colour they took, so they bleed it when cut. Where Xel fights, colour splashes back onto the white.

**Xel** wakes on floor 14 of an office tower with one orange voxel in hand and a plastic wristband: `XEL — 07`. No memory, never speaks.

**VE** is unknown. Orange marks signed `VE` are left on walls along the route: arrows, 3-frame doodles (these are the tutorials), numbers. Old screens and radios sometimes print short lines signed `VE`. Is VE helping, testing, or leading Xel somewhere? Not answered in Chapter 1.

Title: XELVE = XEL + VE. Never stated; the final card of Chapter 1 shows the two signatures side by side.

## Chapter 1 · Trắng (8 levels, same layout as before)
1. **Hành lang tầng 14.** Wake at the lift. Clock stopped 05:47. First orange VE doodle teaches the swipe. Signal 1 on the lift screen.
2. **Văn phòng.** Desks still set for work. A sketch page taped to a monitor: 3×3 grid, orange centre square, signed VE → opens Xưởng. On one desk: a stack of identical wristbands `XEL — 01…06`, all blank white (setup). Signal 2.
3. **Cầu thang.** Orange arrows down. Floor 7 emergency light still on. Signal 3.
4. **Con hẻm.** TV repair shop, every screen static; Bóng nhiễu. One TV shows a frozen frame of the same alley from a high angle, someone watching. Signal 4.
5. **Sảnh — Người gác.** The Bóng copying the night guard's round. When it falls its radio says: "Số 07 đã qua sảnh." Drops VE sketch pages: Bản vẽ Dao, Kiếm (margins: measurements only, signed VE).
6. **Đường cái.** Frozen traffic. Every billboard shows the same countdown, stopped. Bản vẽ Búa at a construction site. Signal 5.
7. **Bãi đỗ xe.** Cars sliding toward the centre, piling. A tall broadcast tower (Đài phát) beyond the car park pulses white, slow.
8. **Boss 2: Đống.** Phase 3 tears open its chest: inside is a **wall of orange VE marks** — hundreds of tally lines, the last group shows 6 crossed out (setup paid: 06 previous attempts). Bộ đàm plays signal 6. Xel looks at the tower. Card: two signatures `XEL` · `VE`, then **Chương 2 · Đài phát — Sắp ra mắt**.
