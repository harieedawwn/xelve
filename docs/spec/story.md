# Story — XELVE

Source of truth for plot, characters, lines. All in-game text goes through `src/i18n/vi.ts`; this file is the script.
Writing rules (from research, see notes.md#story):
- Do → show → tell. Gameplay and rooms carry the story; text is the last layer.
- Xel never speaks (first person). Ve speaks only through old voice messages, notes and sketches.
- Lines are short, plain, spoken Vietnamese. No speeches, no "định mệnh/linh hồn/bóng tối nuốt chửng". No exclamation marks in sad lines.
- Every setup pays off later (table at the end). Never explain the metaphor out loud.

## Premise
At 05:47 one morning the city starts turning white. Not burning, not breaking: colour simply leaves things, and the white spreads.
What the white swallows, it remembers badly. It rebuilds that into **Bóng**: charcoal figures copying the habits of people who used to be there (walking the same corridor, guarding the same desk). They are not people. They hold the colour they swallowed, so when cut they bleed it: deep purple, deep green, deep blue, crimson. Wherever Xel fights, colour is splashed back onto the white. Cleared levels keep those stains on the chapter map. That is the visual progress of the game.

**Title meaning.** XELVE = **Xel** + **Ve**, two siblings. Revealed only at the very end (final title card splits into XEL · VE).

## Characters
- **Xel** — 17. Younger sibling (gender left open; Ve says "em"). Silent. Holds one orange voxel: an acrylic 1-pixel keychain Ve made for Xel's birthday ("pixel đầu tiên của chị"). It is the only thing that kept its colour, and it lets Xel build things out of colour.
- **Ve** — 24. Pixel artist at a small game studio, floor 14 of an office tower. Their mother died a year ago at the city hospital, three weeks after a car accident. Ve was driving; a truck ran a red light, nobody blames her except herself. The sticky note on the dashboard ("Mẹ ơi, con lái chậm thôi mà") was an old joke between them, written long before. Ve has been drawing her face every night since and can no longer get it right. Warm, dry humour, tired.
- **Chú Bảy** — night guard of the tower. Liked Ve, she drew him once. Appears only as the Bóng that copies him (Boss 1) and as objects at his desk.
- **The white (Trắng)** — never a character with lines. In chapter 2 it is revealed to come from Ve: a wish to erase one day, which did not stop at one day.

## Device: voice messages
Xel's phone has 6 unheard voice messages from Ve, sent the night before. The signal comes back a little in each level, one message plays at the level's quiet moment (subtitle only, waveform icon, no portrait). They are the spine of Chapter 1.

## Chapter 1 · Trắng (8 levels)
Route: the office tower → the alley → the main road → the hospital car park. Xel is going to the hospital because of message 4.

1. **Hành lang, tầng 14.** Xel wakes on the floor by the lift (came last night to find Ve, fell asleep waiting). Wall clock stopped at 05:47. Everything white. The orange voxel in hand. On the corridor wall: one of Ve's pixel doodles, still in colour — a stick figure swiping a sword, drawn as 3 frames (this *is* the swipe tutorial). First Bóng shuffles out of a meeting room. Message 1 plays after the fight.
2. **Văn phòng.** Ve's desk. Dozens of sketches of a woman's face; every face rubbed out. A sketchbook page taped to the monitor: a 3×3 grid with an orange square in the middle and the note "bắt đầu từ một ô". Picking it up opens the **Xưởng** (forge). Glass partitions break into Thuỷ tinh. A small framed drawing of an old silver car (setup). Message 2.
3. **Cầu thang thoát hiểm.** 14 floors down. Bóng chạy (runners) come up the stairs. Rusty pipes give Chì, window frames give Nhôm. Message 3 on the landing of floor 7, where the stairwell light is still on. (Unlocks Vô tận.)
4. **Con hẻm sau toà nhà.** An old TV repair shop: all screens show static, and Bóng nhiễu flicker out of them. A welding shop gives Magie. Ve's sticker on a shutter (she grew up nearby). Grid grows to 5×5. Message 4.
5. **Sảnh toà nhà — Boss 1: Người gác.** Back through the lobby to reach the street. Behind the desk: a thermos, a radio, Ve's drawing of Chú Bảy taped up. The Bóng wearing his shape walks his round with a baton, fast and precise. Duel (dodge/parry). When it falls, the radio crackles once: "Tầng mười bốn ổn chưa?" Drops Ve's sketch pages: **Bản vẽ Dao** and **Bản vẽ Kiếm** (her notes in the margin: "cán dài thêm chút, tay Xel to hơn tay chị").
6. **Đường cái.** Frozen traffic, every car white. Bóng khiên carry road signs. Tyres give Cao su, a construction site gives **Bản vẽ Búa** (Ve's note: "cái này nặng lắm, nhưng em thích thì vẽ"). Message 5.
7. **Bãi đỗ xe bệnh viện.** Multi-storey car park. Bóng vỡ (splitters). A refrigerated truck gives Đá khô. Cars are moving on their own, sliding toward the centre of the lot, piling up. The hospital across the road pulses white, slowly, like breathing.
8. **Boss 2: Đống.** The cars become a giant. It throws cars, crushes cover. Xel leaps to cut its face and seams. When it collapses, among the wreck is the old silver car from Ve's framed drawing — their family car. On the dashboard, a sticky note in Ve's hand: "Mẹ ơi, con lái chậm thôi mà." Message 6 plays. Xel looks up at the hospital. Cut to white. Card: **Chương 2 · Ve — Sắp ra mắt**.

## Chapter 2 · Ve (outline, written later)
1. Hospital lobby: ASCII text of erased memories drifts in the air (names, dates).
2. Ward corridor, room 305 where their mother stayed. Ve's chair beside an empty bed, her pencil on the floor.
3. Their old home in the alley: Xel's own childhood drawings on the fridge, which Ve kept. Bản vẽ Găng.
4. Boss 3: **Bản nháp** — a white copy of Xel that Ve once drew. It copies the weapon the player is currently holding (fight your own drawing).
5. The crossroads where the accident happened a year ago, replayed as a frozen white scene. The truth: Ve was not at fault, but she wanted that day gone, and the white took the whole city with it.
6. Vùng trắng: the centre. Bản vẽ Đũa (draw the tip, the spell flies in that shape).
7. Boss 4: **Chân dung** — a giant faceless figure built from all of Ve's failed sketches. Damage does nothing; the player must use the forge to draw colour into its face (forge-as-combat).
8. Ve. No fight. Xel puts the orange voxel in her hand. Colour comes back to the city, stained with every colour spilled on the way: not clean, but full. Ve's last line: "Mặt mẹ đây rồi. Chị nhớ rồi." Title card splits: XEL · VE.

## Text placement in game
- Voice message: bottom-centre subtitle, typed in at reading speed, tap to finish. Max 2 lines.
- Notes and sketches: pick-up shows the object in 3D for 2 s with its handwriting texture; no text box.
- No narrator. No opening crawl. Game starts on the corridor floor, eyes opening (camera script `wake`).
